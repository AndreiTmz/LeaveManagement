A SIMPLE FLOW

From request
to decision.

A small, straightforward workflow designed to keep the employee and manager experience connected.

  1. 01

    Check your balance

    Review available days and choose a leave type.

  2. 02

    Send a request

    Select dates, add a replacement when required, and submit.

  3. 03

    Review in the calendar

    Managers see pending reviews and open a request for details.

  4. 04

    Keep everyone up to date

    The request status and team calendar reflect the decision.
